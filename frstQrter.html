First Quarterly Report

Download MENRO's First Quarter Report to see this vision already in motion. The report highlights real, on the ground progress: the strengthened Municipal Solid Waste Management Board, continuous barangay and school information drives on proper waste disposal, the Waste to Energy partnership with Republic Cement, the Garbage Enzyme liquid waste program, weekly Manila Bay river clean ups, tree growing initiatives, and ongoing upgrades to MRF equipment and personnel welfare. It also candidly reports the challenges MENRO faces, from aging garbage trucks to storage renovations, reflecting the office's commitment to honest, accountable governance. Downloading both documents empowers citizens to stay informed, hold local leadership accountable, and actively join Rizal's mission: Discipline and Be Green, That's the Dream.
