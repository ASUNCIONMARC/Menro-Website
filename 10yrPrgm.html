10 Year Program Plan

Download the 10-Year Municipal Solid Waste Management Plan (2026–2035) and the MENRO First Quarter Report today. Rizal, Laguna's Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office invites every resident, business owner, and barangay leader to be part of the municipality's boldest step yet toward a cleaner, safer, and more sustainable community. This 10-year plan, developed under the Municipal Solid Waste Management Board and chaired by Hon. Mayor Vener P. Muñoz, lays out a clear roadmap for waste segregation at source, expanded collection services, a fully operational Sanitary Landfill and Materials Recovery Facility, and stronger enforcement of the "No Segregation, No Collection" policy under RA 9003. By downloading and reading the plan, citizens gain full transparency into how their municipality intends to manage waste responsibly for the next decade, and how every household's participation, from proper segregation to responsible disposal, directly protects Rizal's environment, public health, and future generations.
