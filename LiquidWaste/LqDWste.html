Liquid Waste Program

Mokusaku, also widely known as wood vinegar or pyroligneous acid, is an organic, reddish-brown liquid produced by condensing the smoke and steam generated during charcoal and biochar production. It is a sustainable, eco-friendly liquid used extensively as a natural fertilizer, pest repellent, and soil enhancer. When burnable biomass such as wood, bamboo, or coconut shells is heated in a low-oxygen chamber, the organic liquids and saps evaporate. The smoke and steam are directed through a cooling tube, condensing back into a liquid. This raw liquid settles for 3 to 6 months before use and contains over 200 organic constituents, including acetic acid, methanol, and phenols.
